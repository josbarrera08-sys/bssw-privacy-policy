PRIVACY POLICY BSSW Reviewer Last updated: September 28, 2026 · Developer: Joseph C. Barrera, RSW · Contact: josbarrera08@gmail.com BSSW Reviewer (also listed on Google Play as "Social Work Licensure Reviewer") is a study app for BS Social Work students and Social Work Licensure Examination (SWLE/LESW) reviewees in the Philippines. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what choices you have — including how to delete your account and data. The app is built to work fully offline for its core study features — quizzes, mock exams, and flashcards — and only reaches the internet for the optional Leaderboard, Feedback, and ad-supported features described below. Information stored only on your device These never leave your device and are not visible to the developer or anyone else: • Your full quiz, mock exam, and flashcard history and progress (Recent Activity, Subject Performance, My Progress, Flashcard History), including the individual answers you chose • Notes and Favorites you attach to questions • App preferences: theme, font, layout, and notification settings You can clear this data at any time from Settings → Data & History → Clear Learning History. Uninstalling the app also removes it. If you sign in with Google, a summary of each quiz and mock exam result is also saved online, as described in the next section. If you never sign in, none of your results leave your device. Information collected if you use optional online features FEATURE WHAT'S COLLECTED WHY Google Sign-In (optional) Your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google To identify your entry on the shared Leaderboard, and to link your quiz and mock exam results to your account Full name and school (signed-in users only) When you sign in, the app asks for your surname, first name, and middle initial (required), and the school you graduated from (optional) So the developer, as the app's administrator and a review instructor, can identify your results when monitoring student progress. Stored privately with your email: only the developer can view them. Leaderboard Display name, photo, chosen avatar, and your aggregate quiz accuracy (not individual question answers). Your display name is the nickname you set; if you haven't set one, the ranking shows your school instead, or your initials if you gave no school. Your full name is never shown. To show a shared ranking of reviewees inside the app Quiz & mock exam results (signed-in users only) For each quiz or mock exam you finish: the subject or exam area, quiz type (quiz or mock exam), score, number correct and unanswered, pass/fail result, time taken, and date and time — stored together with your email address. Your individual answers to each question are not uploaded. So the developer, as the app's administrator and a review instructor, can monitor students' progress and give study support Send Feedback The message you write, an optional name, and — if you're signed in — your account ID So the developer can read and respond to feedback Ads Advertising identifiers and interaction data, collected by Google AdMob per its own privacy practices To show ads that keep the app free YOU'RE ALWAYS IN CONTROL Signing in with Google is entirely optional. It's only needed to appear on the Leaderboard and to have your results saved to your account. Everything else in the app (quizzes, mock exams, flashcards, notes, favorites, progress tracking) works fully without ever signing in or connecting to the internet — and if you don't sign in, no quiz results are collected at all. How your information is used • Leaderboard data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is visible to other app users as a ranking — only your display name, photo, avatar, and aggregate accuracy are shown, never your full name, your email, or your individual quiz answers. • Your full name and school are stored privately in Firebase (Cloud Firestore) with your email address. Your full name is never shown to other app users; only the developer can view it, through the administrator-only screen and progress exports. Your school or initials appear on the Leaderboard only as described above, when you have not set a nickname. • Quiz and mock exam results of signed-in users are stored privately in Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to your email address. They are never shown to other app users — only the developer can view them, through an administrator-only screen in the app, and may export them to a spreadsheet for monitoring student progress. They are not published or sold. If you finish a quiz while offline and signed in, the result is kept on your phone and uploaded automatically once you're back online. • Feedback is stored privately in Firebase and is readable only by the developer — it is never shown to other users. • Ad data is collected and processed by Google AdMob under its own privacy policy. Where required, the app will ask for your consent to personalized ads before showing any. Data sharing Your information is not sold. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the features above: • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — for sign-in, the Leaderboard, quiz and mock exam result history, and Feedback storage • Google AdMob — for showing ads Delete your account and data HOW TO REQUEST DELETION To delete your BSSW Reviewer account and the data associated with it, send an email to josbarrera08@gmail.com from the same Google account email you used to sign in, with the subject line "Delete my BSSW Reviewer account." Include your Leaderboard nickname if you remember it, to help us find your entry faster. Requests are processed within 7 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done. What gets deleted: your Leaderboard entry (display name, avatar/photo, aggregate accuracy, best score and time), your saved quiz and mock exam result history, your full name and school, and the email address stored with them, any Feedback messages linked to your account, and your Google sign-in association with the app's backend. Copies previously exported by the developer for progress monitoring will also be deleted. What is not affected: study data that only lives on your own device (your on-device quiz history and answers, notes, favorites, flashcard progress) is not touched by this process, because it was never sent to us. You can clear that separately, any time, from Settings → Data & History → Clear Learning History, or by uninstalling the app. You do not need to delete the app or your Google account itself to make this request — deleting your BSSW Reviewer data does not affect your Google account or any other app. Locally-stored study data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Leaderboard data, quiz and mock exam result history, and feedback stored in Firebase are otherwise kept only until you request deletion as described above. Children's privacy BSSW Reviewer is intended for college-level Social Work students and licensure reviewees and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. BSSW Reviewer is developed independently by Joseph C. Barrera, RSW, for BS Social Work students and SWLE/LESW reviewees in the Philippines. Questions about this policy, your data, or account deletion requests can be sent to josbarrera08@gmail.com.
